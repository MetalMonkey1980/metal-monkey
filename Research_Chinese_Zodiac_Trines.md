# Chinese Zodiac Trines — Research Notes

## Purpose

This document is a research and comparison document for the Metal Monkey project.

It is not part of the book and does not represent established scientific evidence.
Its purpose is to collect publicly available descriptions of the twelve Chinese zodiac
animals and compare them with the four Trigon patterns currently used in the Metal Monkey model.

The goal is not to prove the Metal Monkey model, but to investigate whether independently
described characteristics show interesting similarities, differences, or contradictions.
