# 🌌 Council of 60 — Conceptual and Structural Map (v2.0)

This document defines the exact coordinate system of the Council of 60. Each of the 60 characters is located at the intersection of four independent layers: Approach Polarity (Yin/Yang), Internal Engine (Trigons), Life Role (Seasonal Intersections), and Space Cultivation (Elements).

---

## 1. ☯️ LAYER 1: Approach Polarity (Yin / Yang)

Determines the primary direction of energy flow and the initial strategy when interacting with reality.

*   **Yang (Active Approach):** Energy directed outward. Expansion, direct action, rapid decision-making, and projecting one's will onto the external world.
*   **Yin (Receptive Approach):** Energy directed inward. Internalization, quiet analysis, deep intuition, and waiting for the right inner impulse.

---

## 2. 🎡 LAYER 2: Internal Engine (The Four Trigons)

Divides the 12 animal archetypes into 4 relational triads based on how their minds process information and what constitutes their primary inner motivation.

### 🏛️ Trigon 1: The Innovators (Rat, Dragon, Monkey)
*   **Core Trait:** A mind constantly running ahead. Strategic thinking, sharp curiosity, and a drive to find novel combinations or clever solutions where others see a dead end.

### 🧠 Trigon 2: The Intellectuals (Ox, Snake, Rooster)
*   **Core Trait:** Pragmatism and depth. Making decisions based on facts and data, high internal organization, and a need to drill beneath the surface to build on solid ground.

### 🐅 Trigon 3: The Independent (Tiger, Horse, Dog)
*   **Core Trait:** A fierce desire for freedom and autonomy. Direct action guided by pure instinct, raw authenticity, high dynamism, and a need for open space to breathe.

### 🤝 Trigon 4: The Diplomats (Rabbit, Goat, Pig)
*   **Core Trait:** A pursuit of harmony and peace. High empathy, deep sensitivity to the emotions of others, aesthetic awareness, and a natural ability to build bridges and smooth over conflicts.

---

## 🏛️ LAYER 3: Life Role (The Three Seasonal Intersections)

This layer divides the 12 animals into 3 cross-cutting groups of 4 animals (exactly one from each trigon). It determines how an archetype naturally responds to time, movement, and life transitions.

### 🎯 Intersection A: Cardinal Signs / Peaks of Seasons
*   **Composition:** Rat (T1), Rooster (T2), Horse (T3), Rabbit (T4)
*   **Core Trait:** Natural leaders and trendsetters. Intensive processing, an inherent drive, and the capacity to withstand immense pressure while holding the steering wheel steady in the middle of a storm.

### 🎡 Intersection B: Mutable Signs / Initiators of Seasons
*   **Composition:** Monkey (T1), Snake (T2), Tiger (T3), Pig (T4)
*   **Core Trait:** Change-makers and transformers. They love movement, flexibility, travel, and starting new projects on a blank canvas. They feel completely at home navigating chaos and transition.

### 🧱 Intersection C: Earth Signs / Guardians of Seasons
*   **Composition:** Dragon (T1), Ox (T2), Dog (T3), Goat (T4)
*   **Core Trait:** Protectors of continuity and stabilizers. Resilient anchors who resist rushed or ungrounded changes. Their role is to give chaos a clear form, anchor moving parts, and safely see things through to completion.

## 🛠️ LAYER 4: Space Cultivation (The Five Elements)

Elements do not define fixed, static personality traits. They represent attraction-based lenses—magnets of attention that determine how a person shapes, organizes, and interacts with the space around them.

### 🌲 Wood (Horizons & Future Growth)
*   **Core Resonance:** *"What could this become tomorrow? What is the trend? Let’s rebuild, innovate, and expand."*
*   **Space Cultivation:** Driven by future potential and constant transformation. The mind naturally focuses on horizons, continuous development, and acquiring new impulses. Stagnation is perceived as suffocation.
*   **Historical Lens Indicator:** Driven by emerging trends and visionary expansion.

### 🔥 Fire (The Spark & Transformation)
*   **Core Resonance:** *"My presence changes the temperature in the room; I cannot leave things cold and stagnant because I live for the moment to ignite people and situations for action, bring pure energy, and with uncompromising passion transform reality right now."*
*   **Space Cultivation:** Driven by instant activation, emotional radiation, and acceleration. The mind refuses to leave spaces nether, neutral, or cold. It injects intense energy, inspiration, or disruptive friction to burn away stagnation.
*   **Historical Lens Examples:** Steve Jobs (Ignition field), Nikola Tesla (Intuitive spark), Jaromír Jágr (Unfading passion), Donald Trump (Disruptive blaze), Václav Klaus (Intellectual heat).

### 🧱 Earth (Foundations & Continuity)
*   **Core Resonance:** *"How do we make this safe, stable, and lasting? Let us build solid ground that所有人 can stand upon."*
*   **Space Cultivation:** Driven by resilience, preservation, and long-term sustainability. The mind naturally focuses on building massive, safe, and durable physical or institutional structures designed to withstand the test of time and provide absolute certainty for future generations.
*   **Historical Lens Examples:** Charles IV (Monuments of stone), Emperor Qin Shi Huang (The Great Wall, unified standards).

### ⚔️ Metal (Structure & Boundaries)
*   **Core Resonance:** *"What is the underlying law and correct structure? Everything must have precise rules to prevent chaos."*
*   **Space Cultivation:** Driven by crisp logic, strict organization, discipline, and sharp boundaries. The mind deconstructs reality into system components, rules, and precision execution. It cuts through chaos like a surgical scalpel.
*   **Historical Lens Examples:** Julius Caesar (Roman law, military legions), Vlasta Burian (Mastering and parodying rigid bureaucratic/military systems through ultra-precise craft).

### 💧 Water (The Flow & Present Process)
*   **Core Resonance:** *"What is flowing right now? Let us fluidly adapt, feel the minor nuances, and organically connect the moving parts."*
*   **Space Cultivation:** Driven by empathy, intuition, and seamless navigation. Unlike Wood, Water does not focus on future trends, but on the living, present process. It views reality as an unbroken organic river. It anchors emotionally in the space and avoids hard barriers, leaving tools where the flow naturally pauses.
*   **Historical Lens Indicator:** Fluid adjustment and quiet human networking.
